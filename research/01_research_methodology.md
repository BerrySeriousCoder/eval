# Research Methodology

Every platform is analysed using the same framework.

## Evidence standard

For profiles researched from 2026-09-11 onward:

- Record the research date and whether the work is documentation-only or includes hands-on validation.
- Cite official documentation, API references, or first-party source code near the claim they support.
- Separate public product behavior from conceptual diagrams and our implementation proposals.
- Treat undocumented capabilities as unknown, rather than absent.
- Label source-access failures and avoid unsupported claims about customer complaints or private architecture.
- Scope prices to the exact feature and date checked; omit unverified numbers.
- End with an implementation lesson or exercise tied to our learning project.

Earlier drafts require revalidation against this standard.
