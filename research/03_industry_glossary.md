## STT

Speech-to-Text

Converts spoken audio into text.

---

## TTS

Text-to-Speech

Converts generated text into speech.

---

## Turn

A single exchange between the user and the assistant.

---

## Interruption (Barge-in)

When the user speaks before the assistant has finished talking.

---

## Tool Call

An external function executed by the conversation engine.

---

## Conversation State

The complete context maintained throughout a call.

---

## Evaluation

The process of measuring conversation quality.

---

## Benchmark

A repeatable collection of scenarios used to compare systems.
